9:00 AM · the practical hour

Know before you go.

The layer TripAdvisor doesn't have. Exact, honest, updated by residents. Breakfast question included: yes, there's still caldo — until eight.

Altitude
2,590 M / 8,497 FT ASL

Take day one slowly. Soup helps, beer doesn't.

Getting there
BOGOTÁ · 3.5–4 H BY BUS

Terminal Norte, roughly every 30 min, 5 AM–7 PM. COP 35,000–45,000.

When to come
MARKET DAY · SATURDAY

Dec–Feb and Jul–Aug are the driest windows.

Weather
7 °C DAWN · 19 °C MIDDAY

High-Andean. Layers, not a parka.

Money
MARKET · CASH ONLY

Cards in most restaurants; ATMs around Plaza de los Libertadores.

Food safety
EAT WHERE THE DRIVERS EAT

The busiest caldo stand is the safest one. Tap water: ask us per neighborhood.
