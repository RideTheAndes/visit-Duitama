Know before you go

The practical layer, checked the same way the partners are.

Altitude
2,590 M / 8,497 FT ASL

Take day one slowly. Soup helps, beer doesn't.

Getting there
BOGOTÁ · 3.5–4 H BY BUS

Terminal Norte, roughly every 30 min, COP 35,000–45,000.

When to come
MARKET DAY · SATURDAY

Dec–Feb and Jul–Aug are the driest windows.

Weather
7 °C DAWN · 19 °C MIDDAY

Layers, not a parka.

Money
MARKET · CASH ONLY

Cards in most restaurants; ATMs around Plaza de los Libertadores.

Food safety
EAT WHERE THE DRIVERS EAT

The busiest caldo stand is the safest one. Tap water: ask us per neighborhood.
