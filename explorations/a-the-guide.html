Know before you go

The practical layer TripAdvisor doesn't have. Exact, honest, updated by residents.

Altitude
2,590 M / 8,497 FT ASL

Take day one slowly. Soup helps, beer doesn't.

Getting there
TERMINAL NORTE · EVERY ~30 MIN · 5 AM–7 PM

190 km northeast of Bogotá via Tunja, 3.5–4 h by bus, COP 35,000–45,000. COP 35–45K

When to come
MARKET DAY · SATURDAY

Saturday if you can. December–February and July–August are the driest windows. DEC–FEB · JUL–AUG

Weather
7 °C DAWN · 19 °C MIDDAY

High-Andean, most days of the year. Bring layers, not a parka.

Money
MARKET · CASH ONLY

Colombian pesos. Cards work in most restaurants; ATMs around Plaza de los Libertadores. ATMS · PLAZA DE LOS LIBERTADORES

Language
ENGLISH · EVERY VERIFIED PARTNER

Spanish on the street. English at every Verified Partner — and from us, any day, on WhatsApp.

Food safety
RULE · EAT WHERE THE DRIVERS EAT

The busiest caldo stand is the safest one. Tap water varies by neighborhood — ask us. TAP WATER · ASK PER BARRIO

04

Real Boyacá

People first, scenery later. These four are why you come.

SLOT 02 · plano medio · doña Carmen sirviendo caldo · luz de puesto, 5:30 AM · 4:3

doña Carmen — the market

SAME CORNER OF THE PLAZA · YEARS TBC

Rib broth, cilantro, a potato that matters. Her pot goes on before the city wakes and is scraped clean by eight.

"If you don't finish the caldo, you don't pay me."

SLOT 03 · plano medio · Julián Tolosa emplatando · luz de ventana · 4:3

Julián Tolosa — Fusionario

TASTING MENU · PÁRAMO INGREDIENTS

Cubios, guatila, feijoa — plated like Bogotá, priced like Boyacá.

"People come for the páramo and stay for the cubios."

SLOT 04 · plano medio · Davide Baiocco estirando pasta · luz de cocina · 4:3

Davide Baiocco — Rugantino di Roma

FRESH PASTA · MADE DAILY

A Roman who chose Duitama. Ask him why he left Rome and he'll answer while kneading.

"Rome taught me pasta. Duitama taught me why I make it."

SLOT 05 · plano medio · Jahir en la huerta de Casa Ambient · media mañana · 4:3

Jahir — Casa Ambient

SUSTAINABLE STAY · GARDEN BREAKFAST

Sustainable rooms on the green edge of town. Breakfast is whatever the garden did that week.

"The garden decides the breakfast, not me."

Move like a local

Boyacá is Colombia's cycling heartland. These are the routes we actually ride and walk — with the food stops that make them worth it.

Hiking Cycling Origin food Sustainable stay Point of interest

La Rusia páramo climb

Road · Hard
25 KM UP +1,100 M

Frailejones at the top; fog most mornings. Agua de panela and farm cheese from the thermos at the summit — bring your own or ask us.

Pueblito Boyacense morning loop

Walk · Easy
4 KM ALMOJÁBANAS WARM UNTIL ~9 AM

Through the replica village and back before the buses arrive. Ends at almojábanas and coffee.

Santa Rosa de Viterbo circuit

Road · Moderate
32 KM ROLLING

Two climbs, one colonial plaza. The mid-ride stop is longaniza santarrosana — it's the point of the ride. LONGANIZA · MID-RIDE
